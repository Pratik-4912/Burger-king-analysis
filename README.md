This project analyzes sales data from a single Burger King outlet for January to March 2026 to understand how revenue changes month by month and which items and categories contribute the most to it. The data comes from two files: menu.csv, which lists 32 menu items with their category and price, and order_details.csv, which records every item sold along with its order ID, date and time. The two tables are joined by matching order_details.item_id to menu.menu_item_id, and revenue is calculated from the menu price of each item sold. Using Python, SQL and Power BI, the analysis covers monthly revenue trends and growth, top and bottom performing items, category-wise revenue share, average order value, peak hours and days, and items frequently bought together. The results are meant to support practical decisions on menu focus, combo offers and staffing.# Burger-king-analysis
